# SAP S/4HANA FI-CO Practice Project

Hands-on practice in SAP S/4HANA Finance (SAP GUI, training system): created a company code and configured enterprise structure, variants, chart of accounts, controlling settings, GL settings and AP/AR foundation.

## What is in this project
- **Organisational structure:** company code, chart of accounts, controlling area
- **Controlling (CO):** cost center and standard cost center hierarchy
- **General Ledger:** GL accounts, 8 postings, cash balance of INR 338,000 reconciled to capital, income and expenses
- **AP/AR foundation:** Customer/Vendor Integration (vendor and customer account groups mapped to Business Partner)
